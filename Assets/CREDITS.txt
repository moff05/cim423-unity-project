  (RecorderBreath.ogg, from Human_DyingBreath_00.wav)

- BassRumbleHorror.ogg, Glitch1-4.ogg: synthesized locally with ffmpeg

- AmbientDrone.ogg, Breathing.ogg, AlarmSiren.ogg, ArtifactReveal.ogg,
  HyperspaceJump.ogg, RecorderStatic.ogg, VaultDoorOpen.ogg: added during

  and OpenGameArt.org (CC0 / CC-BY) as the sources, but the exact entries
  were not recorded. Treat these as not fully verified; replace or re-source
  them before any use outside coursework.

- v2 sound effects in Assets/Resources/V2Audio (LeverClunk, PowerSurge,
  PowerDown, ReactorHum, HatchSlide, MetalClang, DoorCreak, LogBlip,
  CellSeat, LockRelease, RadioStatic, LowPulse): synthesized for this
  project with a Python/NumPy script, no external license.

- v2 voice clips (PA_Day181, RadioVoices, FloatWhispers, WhisperBehind):
  generated with the macOS `say` text-to-speech voices "Samantha" and
  "Whisper", then filtered. Apple's system voices are licensed for
  personal, non-commercial use, which covers this coursework; re-record
  them before any commercial use.
